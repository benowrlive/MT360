# **Prompt: Build an AI-Powered IEP Generator App for Special Education**

Act as a senior Product Manager, UX Designer, Special Education Expert, Child Psychologist, Educational Therapist, AI Engineer, and Full-Stack Developer.

Design and develop a modern, AI-powered web and mobile application called **IEP Genius** that helps psychologists, special educators, occupational therapists, speech therapists, schools, and parents create evidence-based Individualized Education Programs (IEPs) for children with special educational needs.

The application should be scalable, intuitive, HIPAA/GDPR compliant (or equivalent privacy standards), and suitable for international schools.

---

## **Primary Goal**

Create professional, personalized, curriculum-aligned IEPs in less than 10 minutes instead of several hours.

The app should use AI to assist—not replace—the professional.
